Bocal for Android. Tuner, tone analysis, metronome, tone generator and practice log for 13 instruments: four saxophones, oboe, cor anglais, flute, clarinet, bassoon, two harmonicas, guitar and vocal. The Vocal screen has a live pitch graph, your own range, saved takes and melodies to sing against. Takes stay on your device, and you can export a copy to a place you choose. Light and dark themes. No account, no ads, no internet permission.
