First release on Google Play. Tuner, tone analysis, metronome, tone generator and practice log for 13 instruments: four saxophones, oboe, cor anglais, flute, clarinet, bassoon, two harmonicas, guitar and voice. The Vocal stage has a live pitch graph, your own range, saved takes and melodies to sing against. Takes stay on your device and export with Save As. Light and dark themes. No account, no ads, no internet permission.
