Rebuilt on the current web app: microphone tuning with reference-pitch and
custom temperaments, tone analysis with harmonics and takes, a metronome with
sequences and practice tracking, tone-generator exercises, fingering charts
for saxophone, clarinet, flute, oboe and bassoon, and a 3D lab for saxophone
and oboe -- all running on device, in light or dark theme, with the screen
kept awake while the tuner or metronome is running.
