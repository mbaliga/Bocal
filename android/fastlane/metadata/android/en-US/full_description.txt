Bocal is a local-first practice companion for saxophone and other woodwinds, combining tuning, tone analysis, pulse work, practice tracking and instrument learning.

PRACTICE TOOLS
• Chromatic tuner with reference pitch, temperament, sensitivity, damping and precision controls.
• Metronome, count-ins, drills, drone/reference tones and practice plans.
• Waveform, spectrum and harmonics views, plus local recording, import, listen-back, rename, delete and export.
• Practice history and skill evidence stored locally on your device.

INSTRUMENT LEARNING
• Alto saxophone: licensed detailed 3D model with interactive fingering tools.
• Tenor, soprano and baritone saxophone: transposition-aware fingering practice shown on the alto model.
• Oboe: licensed 3D anatomy model plus fingering chart.
• Cor anglais: oboe-based proxy model/chart.
• Flute, clarinet and bassoon: 2D fingering charts.
• Guitar: string tuner and chord/follow tools.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Pitch and tone analysis runs on your device.
• Bocal does not use accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
• Audio is not recorded unless you explicitly press Record. Saved takes stay in app-private storage until you delete or export them.
• Bocal does not upload your recordings or practice history.

Bocal requires microphone access for tuning and live analysis. Fingering data and model touch mappings are educational tools and are not yet specialist-certified.
