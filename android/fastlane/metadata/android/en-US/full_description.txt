A local-first practice companion for saxophone, woodwinds and guitar. Tuning, tone
and daily practice, with instrument-specific learning tools that say clearly what
each instrument supports.

WHAT IT COVERS
• Tuning, with the pitch read from your own playing through the microphone.
• Tone work, so you can hear and see what your sound is actually doing.
• Practice workflows organised as sessions rather than a pile of exercises.
• Instrument learning: a 3D fingering trainer for the saxophone family, a 3D
  anatomy preview for oboe and cor anglais, 2D fingering charts for flute,
  clarinet and bassoon, and a chord/fretboard studio for guitar.

WHY WOODWINDS SPECIFICALLY
Intonation on a saxophone is a conversation between embouchure, air and the
instrument, and a generic chromatic tuner tells you almost nothing about it. Bocal
is built for the family rather than adapted to it.

ON YOUR DEVICE
• Pitch detection runs locally. Your audio is analysed in real time and is not
  recorded or uploaded.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your practice history and recorded takes stay on your device. Takes use local
  browser storage when available and can be deleted or downloaded by you.

Bocal needs your microphone to tune and analyse your playing; that is how the
app works.

The fingering charts and saxophone altissimo routes are educational references, not
teacher certification; they are labelled until a qualified player reviews them.
