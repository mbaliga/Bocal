Bocal has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and this version does not ask for the internet permission. Your playing is analysed on your device and is not uploaded.

The tuner and the live analysis views do not record audio. When a tuner session has enough steady readings, the tuner keeps a short summary on this device (the notes heard and how close they were) for your practice log and skill score. The live analysis views keep nothing unless you record a take. Audio is saved only when you press Record take or import an audio file. A saved take stays on your device until you delete it, and you can export a copy.

TUNING
• Pitch read from your playing or singing through the microphone, for 13 instruments
• Written pitch or concert pitch for transposing instruments
• Reference pitch from 415 to 466 Hz, and 11 temperaments including a custom one
• Note names as letters, do re mi, sargam or on a staff
• Tone generator for notes, intervals and chords, with exercises you can save

TONE AND PRACTICE
• Live waveform, spectrum, harmonics and spectrogram views
• Record takes, tag them, compare two of them, or import an audio file
• Turn a single-line audio file into notes and export MIDI or MusicXML
• Metronome with a gap trainer, saved presets, sequences and a drone
• Practice timer, practice log and weekly goal
• A repeat-after-me drill: Bocal plays a short phrase and shows whether you matched it

INSTRUMENTS
• Saxophone (soprano, alto, tenor, baritone): a fingering trainer and a 2D chart. The 3D model is an alto saxophone and is shown for all four, because it is the only saxophone model in the app.
• Oboe: a 3D model with part names, and a fingering chart. The cor anglais uses the oboe model and the oboe chart.
• Flute, clarinet and bassoon: 2D fingering charts, no 3D model
• Diatonic harmonica (12 keys) and chromatic harmonica: hole charts
• Guitar: a string tuner with three tunings, six chord diagrams and a chord player that can wait for your root note
• Vocal: a live pitch graph, a range you record yourself, guided warm-ups, saved takes, and melodies to sing against

WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM
• It does not judge your tone colour, expression or singing technique. The Practice screen shows a score built only from your pitch accuracy, steadiness, rhythm taps, fingering checks and the range of notes the tuner has heard, all recorded on your device.
• Bocal does not claim that a teacher has reviewed any fingering chart. Charts follow published references, saxophone altissimo fingerings are marked Unverified, and the harmonica charts are computed from standard layouts.
• Where an instrument borrows another instrument's 3D model, the app says so on that screen.

Bocal needs your microphone to hear you play. It uses vibration only if you switch on Feel the beat in the metronome. It needs Android 8.0 or newer and Android System WebView 69 or newer.
