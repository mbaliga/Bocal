# Privacy Policy — Bocal

> **This is the working copy, not the hosted one.** The URL Play Console points at
> is **https://asystemofcells.com/bocal/privacy**. Keep the two in sync by hand.

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

Bocal is a music practice app for saxophone and other woodwinds (package
`com.bocal.music`), made by A System of Cells. It listens to you play, so this
policy is specific about what happens to that audio.

## The short version

Bocal has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. **Your playing
is analysed in real time on your device and is never uploaded.** If you choose to
record a take, it stays on your device.

## The microphone

Bocal listens through your microphone in order to detect pitch and analyse your
tone. That is how a tuner works, and it is the core function of the app.

- Audio is processed **as it arrives**, on your device. The tuner and tone
  analyser keep nothing.
- **Recording happens only when you press Record.** The Analyze screen lets you
  record short takes to listen back to. Those files are kept in the app's private
  storage on your device, you can delete them at any time, and they are removed
  when you uninstall.
- **Nothing is transmitted.** There is no server of ours, and nothing is uploaded.
- The app listens only while you are using a feature that needs it, and you can
  revoke the permission at any time in Android's settings.

## What is stored, and where

Your practice history, session notes and settings are stored in the app's private
storage on your device. Uninstalling Bocal deletes all of it.

## What is sent off your device, and to whom

**Nothing.**

## Permissions, and why each exists

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `RECORD_AUDIO` | To hear the note you are playing, so the app can show you your pitch and tone. Analysed live; recorded only when you press Record, and only to your device. |
| `VIBRATE` | Metronome and interaction feedback. |

## Children

Bocal is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone,
including children.
